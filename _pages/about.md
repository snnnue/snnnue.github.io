I'm an undergraduate student in Life Sciences (Physiology) at the University of Toronto, entering my fourth year. I work as a volunteer researcher in the [Josselyn-Frankland Lab](https://www.jflab.ca/) at SickKids Research Institute (PGCRL), studying the neuroscience of memory and engrams under Prof. Sheena Josselyn, through the PSL498Y/499Y supervised research program.

Before joining the JF Lab, I did research at KAIST's Memory Biology Lab. I'm also the founder of ARC (Association for Research in Clinical Science), a University of Toronto undergraduate club connecting Korean-Canadian students with research and clinical career opportunities.

I'm broadly interested in how memories are encoded, stored, and retrieved at the level of neural circuits, and in the path from undergraduate research to a career in science and medicine.
